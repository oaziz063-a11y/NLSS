    Questions

    FAQ

    How often do the live numbers update?

    Every minute while this tab is open. Updates pause while the tab is hidden and resume when you come back. Press Refresh for an immediate update.

    Why does it say "Live data unavailable"?

    This page couldn't reach Roblox's public API from your browser. That can come from a network restriction or a brief outage. Try Refresh in a moment.

    What does this page store on my device?

    Two things, both in your browser only: your theme choice and your best reflex score. The page has no analytics, accounts or sign-ins.

    Is the reflex test part of the game?

    No. It's a small warm-up built into this page so you have something to do while you wait for a match.

    How do I share NLSS?

    Use the Share button in the footer. On phones it opens your share menu. Elsewhere it copies the page link.
